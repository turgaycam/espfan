Distance Sales Agreement

This agreement applies only to consumers buying for non-commercial purposes. Business and international buyers are subject to the Terms of Use and Sale.

Article 1 – Parties

Seller: MUSTAFA CEYLAN ENDÜSTRİ A.Ş., K.O.S.B. 3. Sk., No: 7, 42250 Selçuklu / Konya, Türkiye. Further details are given in the Legal Notice.

Buyer: The individual identified by the registered account details at the time of the order.

Article 2 – Subject

The subject of this agreement is the sale and delivery of the goods ordered electronically on the website, whose characteristics and price are stated in the order summary, in accordance with Law No. 6502 and the Distance Contracts Regulation.

Article 3 – Goods, price and payment

Product details, quantity, VAT-inclusive price, shipping cost and total are stated in the order summary and confirmation and form an integral part of this agreement. For euro-based items the CBRT selling rate fixed at payment is applied and recorded with the order. Payment is made in advance by card with 3D Secure. If payment is not completed within 30 minutes, the order is cancelled and no contract is formed.

Article 4 – Delivery

Goods are delivered to the buyer or a person designated by the buyer within 30 days of the order at the latest. For collection from our plant the date is agreed between the parties. The buyer checks the packaging on delivery and has a damage report drawn up by the carrier if the parcel is damaged. The seller bears the risk until delivery to the buyer.

Article 5 – Seller's obligations

  • To deliver goods that match the order and, for used goods, the stated condition definition,
  • To supply the manual, test record and warranty certificate where applicable,
  • To issue and send the invoice,
  • If the goods cannot be supplied, to inform the buyer within 3 days and refund the amount within 14 days.

Article 6 – Buyer's obligations

  • To provide accurate delivery and billing details,
  • To use the goods in line with the manual and safety instructions and have industrial equipment installed by competent persons,
  • To keep account credentials secure.

Article 7 – Right of withdrawal

The buyer may withdraw within 14 days of delivery without giving reasons and without penalty, by written notice or notice on a durable medium. The seller refunds all payments including delivery costs within 14 days of receiving the notice; the buyer returns the goods within 10 days. Exceptions and the return procedure are listed under Returns and Withdrawal.

Article 8 – Defective goods

For defective goods the buyer may exercise the rights under Article 11 of Law No. 6502. Liability for defects is subject to a two-year limitation period from delivery, even if the defect appears later, except where the seller concealed the defect through gross fault or fraud. Defects expressly disclosed before the sale of used goods are not defects. See the Warranty Terms.

Article 9 – Personal data

Personal data are processed as described in the Personal Data Protection Notice and the Privacy Policy.

Article 10 – Disputes and entry into force

Consumer arbitration committees within the monetary limits, and consumer courts above them, have jurisdiction. The agreement is concluded when the buyer accepts the Pre-Contractual Information Form and this agreement at checkout and completes payment. The text and order details are sent to the buyer on a durable medium and kept by the seller for the period required by law.
