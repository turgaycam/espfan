Personal Data Protection Notice (KVKK)

This notice is provided under Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK). Buyers in the EU/EEA should also read our Privacy Policy (GDPR).

1. Data controller

MUSTAFA CEYLAN ENDÜSTRİ A.Ş., K.O.S.B. 3. Sk., No: 7, 42250 Selçuklu / Konya, Türkiye. Contact details are in the Legal Notice.

2. Data we process

  • Identity: name; for sole proprietorships, the Turkish ID number used as tax number.
  • Contact: e-mail, phone, delivery and billing address.
  • Customer transactions: orders, cart, quote requests and feedback.
  • Financial: invoice details, tax office and number, VAT ID, payment result and the last four card digits (the full card number is never stored).
  • Security: IP address, session cookie, sign-in attempts.
  • Marketing: commercial message preferences, only if you consented.

4. Collection method

Data are collected electronically through the registration, order, quotation and feedback forms on the website and through cookies.

5. Transfers

Data may be transferred, limited to these purposes, to carriers and logistics providers, payment institutions and banks, financial advisers and auditors, IT service providers and, on request, competent public authorities. Transfers abroad rely on an adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards such as standard contracts under KVKK Article 9; transfers to carriers and customs brokers in the destination country are necessary to perform the contract (Article 9/6-b). In other occasional cases without such safeguards, your explicit consent is requested.

6. Retention

  • Invoices, orders and commercial records: 10 years from the end of the relevant year (TTK Article 82, Tax Procedure Law Article 253).
  • Account data: until the account is closed, then for applicable limitation periods.
  • Security logs: for the period required under Law No. 5651 and related legislation.
  • Marketing consent: until withdrawn; opt-outs are recorded in the Message Management System (İYS).

7. Your rights (KVKK Article 11)

You may ask whether your data are processed and request information, learn the purpose and whether data are used accordingly, know the third parties to whom data are transferred, request rectification, erasure or destruction under Article 7 and notification of these to recipients, object to adverse results arising solely from automated analysis, and claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

8. Applications

Send your request in writing to our address, to our registered e-mail (KEP) address, or by e-mail from the address registered with us, in accordance with the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles of Applications to the Data Controller. Requests are answered free of charge within 30 days; where the action entails an additional cost, the fee set by the Board may apply.
