Pre-Contractual Information Form

This form is provided to consumers (individual buyers) before the contract is concluded, under Turkish Consumer Protection Law No. 6502 and the Distance Contracts Regulation. Business and international buyers are subject to the Terms of Use and Sale.

1. Seller

MUSTAFA CEYLAN ENDÜSTRİ A.Ş. (Ceylan Robot), K.O.S.B. 3. Sk., No: 7, 42250 Selçuklu / Konya, Türkiye. Registration number, phone, e-mail and registered e-mail (KEP) address are listed in the Legal Notice.

2. Main characteristics of the goods

The product name, stock code (SKU), manufacturer code, quantity and condition (new, used – tested, used – as is) are shown on the product page and in the order summary at checkout. Condition definitions for used goods are given in the Warranty Terms. Defects and signs of use expressly stated on the product page form part of the description of the goods.

3. Price and payment

Prices are shown in Turkish lira including VAT. For euro-based items the lira amount is calculated with the Central Bank of the Republic of Türkiye (CBRT) selling rate and fixed at checkout; if the rate changes, the new amount is presented for your approval before payment. Shipping is shown as a separate line.

Payment is taken in advance by debit or credit card with 3D Secure. Card details are not stored by the seller; only the last four digits are kept with the order. Orders not paid within 30 minutes are cancelled automatically.

4. Delivery

Goods are shipped to your address in Türkiye or collected from our plant in Konya. Delivery takes place within 30 days of the order at the latest. Lead times for items not in stock are confirmed after the order; if the 30-day period would be exceeded you may terminate the contract and the amount paid is refunded within 14 days.

5. Right of withdrawal

You may withdraw from the contract within 14 days of receiving the goods without giving any reason and without penalty. For goods delivered separately, the period starts with delivery of the last item. A written notice or a notice on a durable medium such as e-mail within this period is sufficient.

All payments, including delivery costs, are refunded within 14 days of receipt of your notice using the original payment method. You must return the goods within 10 days of your notice. Returns made with our contracted carrier are free of charge. See Returns and Withdrawal for details.

6. Exceptions to the right of withdrawal

  • Goods made, programmed or assembled to your specifications (for example a controller or robot cell configured for you),
  • Goods which, by their nature, are inseparably mixed with other items after delivery,
  • Software licences and digital content once unsealed.

The buyer is liable for any diminished value resulting from handling or installation beyond what is necessary to establish the nature of the goods.

7. Defective goods and complaints

For defective goods you may exercise your statutory rights under Article 11 of Law No. 6502 (withdrawal, price reduction, free repair or replacement). These rights also apply to used goods; however, defects expressly disclosed before the sale and normal wear appropriate to the age of the item are not defects.

8. Dispute resolution

Complaints may be submitted to the consumer arbitration committee within the monetary limits announced annually by the Ministry of Trade, and to consumer courts above those limits. Where required by law, mediation must be attempted before filing a lawsuit.

9. Acceptance

By ticking the box at checkout you confirm that you have read this form and received the above information before concluding the contract. The form and order details are sent to you on a durable medium with the order confirmation.
