Returns and Right of Withdrawal

Individual consumers have a 14-day right of withdrawal. Business (commercial) purchases carry no right of withdrawal; returns are possible only under the contractual terms below.

A. Individual (consumer) buyers

1. 14-day right of withdrawal

You may withdraw within 14 days of receiving the goods without giving reasons. For goods delivered separately, the period starts with delivery of the last item.

2. How to withdraw

  1. Notify us in writing or by e-mail (see the Legal Notice), stating your order number and the item. No reason is required.
  2. Return the goods within 10 days of your notice, if possible with original packaging, accessories and documents, using the contracted carrier we name.
  3. All payments including delivery costs are refunded to your card within 14 days of receipt of your notice. The refund may be withheld until the goods are received or proof of dispatch is provided.

3. Return costs

Returns with our contracted carrier are free of charge; if you choose another carrier, the cost is yours. For heavy or bulky items (e.g. robots, positioners) return transport is arranged together with the seller.

4. Exceptions

  • Goods made, programmed or assembled to your specifications,
  • Unsealed software licences and digital content,
  • Goods inseparably mixed with other items after delivery.

5. Defective goods

Independently of the withdrawal period, your statutory rights for defective goods under Law No. 6502 apply for two years. Return transport of defective goods is paid by the seller.

B. Business buyers (B2B)

For purchases made for commercial or professional purposes, Law No. 6502 does not apply and there is no right of withdrawal. The relationship is governed by the Turkish Code of Obligations (TBK), the Turkish Commercial Code (TTK) and the Terms of Use and Sale.

  • Inspection and notice: The buyer must inspect the goods on delivery (TBK Article 223, TTK Article 23). Obvious defects must be notified in writing within {{ acik_ayip_gun }} days of delivery, defects that are not obvious within 8 days following inspection, and hidden defects that cannot be found by ordinary inspection as soon as they appear. Goods are deemed accepted for defects not notified in time.
  • Wrong or short delivery: Items not matching the order are replaced or refunded free of charge, including transport.
  • Contractual returns: Any other return requires the seller's written approval. If approved, unopened and unused standard items may be returned subject to a restocking deduction stated at approval.
  • Refund of bank transfer payments: For business and international purchases, amounts paid by bank transfer are refunded by bank transfer to the account the payment came from (indicative).
  • Non-returnable: items configured, programmed or manufactured for the buyer and items sold "used – as is".

C. International buyers

International deliveries are made DAP (Incoterms 2020); customs duties, import taxes and charges in the destination country are borne by the buyer. Defects must be notified within the periods in section B. Unless the seller is at fault, return freight and export/import formalities are borne by the buyer. Taxes and duties paid in the destination country are not refunded by the seller.

D. Used goods

The definitions "used – tested" and "used – as is" and the related warranty terms are given in the Warranty Terms. Consumers' withdrawal and statutory defect rights also apply to used goods.
