Explicit Consent Text

This text covers only the processing described in the Personal Data Protection Notice whose legal ground is explicit consent. Giving consent is optional; refusing it does not prevent you from buying or requesting a quote.

1. Campaign and product announcements

If you tick the relevant box at registration or in your account settings, your name, e-mail, phone and order history are processed to prepare and send relevant product, campaign and used-stock announcements. The box is unticked by default.

2. Commercial electronic messages (Law No. 6563 and İYS)

Commercial e-mails, text messages or calls are sent to consumers only with prior consent, which is recorded in the Turkish Message Management System (İYS). You can opt out free of charge via the link in every message, through İYS or by writing to us; opt-outs are processed within three working days. Messages to merchants and tradespeople may be sent without prior consent where the law allows; the right to opt out still applies.

3. Transfers abroad

Transfers to e-mail, hosting and cloud providers abroad rely primarily on an adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards such as standard contracts under KVKK Article 9. Where no such safeguard exists and the transfer is occasional, your explicit consent is requested after you are informed of the possible risks.

4. Withdrawing consent

You may withdraw your consent at any time in your account settings or via the contact channels in the Legal Notice. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before it.

5. Declaration

By ticking the relevant box I confirm that I have read the Personal Data Protection Notice and that I give my consent freely, specifically and on an informed basis to the processing of my personal data for the purposes stated above.
