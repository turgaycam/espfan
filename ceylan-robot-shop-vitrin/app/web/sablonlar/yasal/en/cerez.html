This website uses only first-party cookies that are strictly necessary for it to work. No analytics, advertising or third-party tracking cookies are used.

1. What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file saved in your browser by a website. Cookies keep you signed in, remember your cart and protect forms against misuse.

Strictly necessary cookies are required to provide the service you request and are used without consent on the grounds of contract performance and legitimate interest (KVKK Article 5/2-c and 5/2-f; GDPR Article 6(1)(b) and (f), in line with Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive). The Turkish Data Protection Authority's guidance on cookies has been taken into account. Should analytics or marketing cookies be introduced, they will be activated only with your consent and this policy will be updated.

4. Product images

Product images are loaded from our company website www.ceylanrobot.com. Your browser technically transmits your IP address to that server; it does not set cookies.

5. Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. If you block strictly necessary cookies, sign-in, cart and checkout will not work. For your data protection rights see the Personal Data Protection Notice and the Privacy Policy.
