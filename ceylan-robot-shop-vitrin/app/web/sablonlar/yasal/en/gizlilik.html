The controller is established in Türkiye, for which no European Commission adequacy decision exists. Your data are transferred to Türkiye to the extent necessary to perform the contract (Art. 49(1)(b)); transfers involving service providers use the Commission's standard contractual clauses (2021/914).

6. Retention

Commercial and tax records are kept for the statutory periods (up to 10 years under Turkish law), account data until the account is closed, and marketing data until consent is withdrawn.

7. Your rights

You have the rights of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). Where processing is based on consent you may withdraw it at any time. You may lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority of the Member State where you live or work. No decisions based solely on automated processing are taken about you (Art. 22).

Send requests to the e-mail address in the Legal Notice. We respond within one month.
