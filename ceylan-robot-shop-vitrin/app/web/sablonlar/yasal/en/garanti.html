Warranty Terms

Warranty coverage depends on the condition of the item (new / used – tested / used (general) / used – as is) and on whether the buyer is a consumer or a business. The condition is shown as a badge on the product page.

1. Condition definitions

New
Unused item in original packaging. The manufacturer's warranty applies.
Used – tested
Previously used item that passed visual inspection, power-up and basic function tests in the Ceylan Robot workshop before dispatch. The test scope and result are stated on the product page and in the test record supplied with the item. Cosmetic signs of use may be present.
Used (general)
Previously used item whose test status is not separately recorded in the source. Photos/a test report are shared before sale; the warranty period is stated on the quotation and the invoice (indicative).
Used – as is
Previously used item sold in its current condition, without or with only partial function testing. Known defects are stated on the product page. Suitable for spare-part or repair purposes.
Not specified
Condition not yet recorded; please confirm with our sales team before ordering.

2. Individual (consumer) buyers

  • New items carry the manufacturer's warranty; for goods that must be sold with a warranty certificate, the period is at least two years.
  • For all items, including used ones, the statutory defect provisions of Law No. 6502 apply, with a two-year limitation period from delivery.
  • The words "as is" do not remove a consumer's statutory rights; however, defects expressly disclosed on the product page before the sale and normal wear appropriate to the item's age are not defects.
  • For "used – tested" items the seller additionally undertakes for 6 months from delivery that the functions listed in the test record will work. This period will be confirmed in the legal and commercial approval process.
  • Repairs under warranty are completed within 20 working days at most.

3. Business buyers (B2B)

  • New: The seller's liability is limited to the terms and duration of the manufacturer's warranty.
  • Used – tested: 6-month function warranty from delivery, limited to repair or replacement and, where neither is possible, a refund.
  • Used (general): The warranty period and scope are stated on the quotation and the invoice (indicative); photos/a test report are shared before sale.
  • Used – as is: The seller's liability for defects is excluded by contract. The buyer accepts the item in its current condition, having been given the opportunity to inspect it. The exclusion does not apply where the seller fraudulently concealed a defect or acted with gross fault (Turkish Code of Obligations, Article 221).
  • Indirect loss: The seller is not liable for indirect loss such as lost production, lost profit or downtime, except in cases of intent or gross negligence (TBK Article 115).
  • Inspection and notice: The periods of TTK Article 23 apply; see Returns and Withdrawal.

4. Exclusions

  • Installation, repair or interference by unauthorised persons,
  • Use contrary to the manual, overload, collision, unsuitable power supply or environment,
  • Consumables and wear parts (cables, belts, batteries, seals, filters, brushes, etc.),
  • Software, parameter or programming changes made by the buyer,
  • Damage from transport or storage by the buyer after delivery.

5. Making a claim

Contact our technical support with the order number, serial number, a description of the fault and, if possible, photos or video (contact details in the Legal Notice). Diagnosis is carried out remotely or in our workshop. Transport is paid by the seller for warranty work and by the buyer otherwise; a quotation is given before any non-warranty repair.
